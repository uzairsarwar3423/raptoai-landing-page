## Introduction: Why Traditional Meeting Tools Fail

Every week, high-growth engineering and product squads spend dozens of hours in standups, sprint planning sessions, architecture reviews, and backlog groomings. During these discussions, critical decisions are agreed upon and verbal commitments are made:

> *"I will push the database migration script by Thursday afternoon."*  
> *"I'll investigate the Redis connection timeout bug and update the Linear ticket."*  
> *"Let's prepare the API docs before Monday's customer demo."*

Yet, industry data reveals that **over 70% of spoken meeting action items are never executed or tracked**. 

The root cause isn't developer negligence—it is the **Accountability Gap**. Traditional meeting transcription tools (such as Otter, Fireflies, or Zoom AI Companion) act as passive recording vaults. They generate bloated, 5-page generic summaries that get dumped into Slack channels or email threads where nobody reads them. They treat every meeting as an ephemeral, isolated event, completely blind to what was promised in yesterday's standup.

**Rapto AI** was engineered specifically to solve this breakdown. Instead of generating passive transcripts, Rapto functions as an **autonomous meeting accountability engine**. It captures audio, extracts structured commitments (Actor, Action, Deadline, Confidence Score), syncs them bi-directionally to developer tools like Linear and Jira, and maintains a **Cross-Meeting Memory Graph** to verify whether yesterday's promises were actually shipped today.

This guide provides a comprehensive, step-by-step walkthrough on **how to get started and onboard with Rapto AI**—taking your squad from initial registration to an active, automated accountability workflow in under 4 minutes.

## 5. Your First Session: The End-to-End Meeting Workflow

Now that your workspace and integrations are active, let us walk through an actual meeting lifecycle.

```
===========================================================================================
                               END-TO-END MEETING WORKFLOW
===========================================================================================

 [ Step 1: Schedule or Add ]   Calendar event automatically schedules Rapto worker, 
                               or click "+ Add meeting" to paste an ad-hoc conference URL.

 [ Step 2: Speak Commitments ] Team conducts standup. Spoken commitments are made:
                               "I'll patch the auth token leak by 3 PM."

 [ Step 3: AI Processing ]     Audio pipeline diarizes speakers, cleans filler words, 
                               and extracts structured commitments with confidence scores.

 [ Step 4: Issue Sync ]        Commitments push to Linear/Jira and broadcast to Slack.

 [ Step 5: Next Meeting ]      Teammate says: "I pushed the auth patch yesterday."
                               Cross-Meeting Memory Engine resolves the previous commitment!
===========================================================================================
```

### Step 1: Ingesting the Meeting
* **Automated Mode**: Because Google Calendar is connected, Rapto automatically dispatches an ingestion worker 2 minutes prior to the start of your scheduled standup.
* **Ad-Hoc / Manual Mode**: If you have an unscheduled call, click the **+ Add meeting** button in the dashboard header, paste your Zoom/Meet URL, and click **Dispatch Bot**. The worker joins within 15 seconds.

### Step 2: Conducting the Discussion
Conduct your meeting naturally. You do not need to alter your speaking style or use special voice commands. Rapto's acoustic diarization models identify individual speakers based on vocal timbre and conference metadata.

### Step 3: Automated Processing (< 45 Seconds)
Within 45 seconds of the call ending, Rapto AI executes its multi-stage pipeline:
1. **Diarization & Transcription**: Generates a timestamped, speaker-attributed transcript.
2. **Divergent Filler Cleanup**: Removes speech artifacts (`um`, `like`, `you know`, repeated words).
3. **Structured NLP Extraction**: Identifies explicit actionable promises, extracting:
   * **Assignee**: The individual responsible for delivery.
   * **Commitment Action**: The specific technical deliverable.
   * **Deadline**: Extracted relative or absolute date.
   * **Confidence Score**: Algorithmic certainty threshold (0.0 to 1.0).

### Step 4: Cross-Meeting Resolution in Action
During your next standup, when Sarah Chen speaks:
> *"The Redis connection pooling script was deployed yesterday and cache hit rates jumped to 96%."*

Rapto AI's Cross-Meeting Memory Engine matches the semantic vector of Sarah's sentence against her open commitment from the previous meeting, evaluates the confidence score (0.92 > 0.65 threshold), and automatically updates the state of `ENG-104` to **RESOLVED**!

## Common Onboarding Issues & Troubleshooting

If you encounter an unexpected state during onboarding, consult the resolution matrix below:

### 1. Calendar Events Not Appearing in Dashboard
* **Symptom**: You connected Google Calendar, but the Upcoming Meetings widget is empty.
* **Cause**: Calendar sync occurs periodically on a 5-minute polling interval, or the event lacks a recognized video conference URL.
* **Fix**: Ensure your calendar event contains a valid Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams link in the location or conference field. You can also trigger an immediate manual refresh by reloading the dashboard.

### 2. Video Conference Bot Not Admitted to Call
* **Symptom**: The Rapto worker waits in the video conference waiting room.
* **Cause**: Your Zoom or Teams security policy requires the meeting host to manually admit external guests from the lobby.
* **Fix**: The call host must click **Admit** when the Rapto worker requests entry. To streamline this, enterprise workspace admins can whitelist the Rapto bot identity in Zoom / Teams account settings.

### 3. Teammates Did Not Receive Invite Emails
* **Symptom**: Coworkers have not received their invitation links.
* **Cause**: Enterprise email filters or spam firewalls intercepted the automated transactional email.
* **Fix**: Have teammates check their spam or quarantine folder. Alternatively, workspace Admins can navigate to **Settings → Members**, click **Copy Invite Link**, and share the URL directly via Slack or direct message.

### 4. Linear / Jira Issues Failing to Sync
* **Symptom**: Action items appear in Rapto, but no corresponding tickets appear in Linear or Jira.
* **Cause**: Missing write permissions or required custom fields in your Jira project schema.
* **Fix**: Visit **Settings → Integrations**, click **Test Connection** on your Jira/Linear card, and ensure your integration user has permission to create issues within the selected project key.
